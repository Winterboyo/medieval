# Ideas and pending design changes

The first list is parked. The 2026-10-01 entries below are user-chosen directions that still need separate specifications and build tasks; `DESIGN.md` decides their current scope.

- **Ladders over the walls** (2026-09-30). Offered as a way into a castle alongside the ram and the siege cannon; not chosen. Would need climbing, exposure while climbing, and a counter for defenders.
- **Attackers turning captured cannons** (2026-09-30). Defensive cannons are defenders-only for now.

## 2026-10-01 design changes awaiting their own build tasks

- **Village specialists in the first playable siege.** The user chose four crafting-only specialists in separate buildings: blacksmith/forge, fletcher, carpenter, and alchemist. The old ranks, theft, and negotiation loop are not part of that choice. Define recipes and interactions in DESIGN.md before implementation.
- **Fixed defensive ballista.** The user parked the movable siege cannon and chose a ballista to defend against players and siege tools. Placement, HP, firing rules, and cost still need a design decision; no weapon art or gameplay is authorized by this note alone.
- **Tunnel placement revision.** The entrance belongs outside each castle in a cover zone; the exit is inside, far from both the stockpile and Keep. Its geometry must first be added to an approved layout revision.
- **Additional attacker siege tools.** The user wants tools beyond the ram eventually. The ram is the first dedicated tool for gates and walls; hand-tool wall damage remains inefficient. Names, targets, and mechanics for the others are not chosen.

## Later specialist ideas — not in the first playable siege

- **Mason:** a possible stoneworking and wall-repair role. Decide whether it adds enough beyond direct player repairs before including it.
- **Cook:** a possible use for Grain through food or stamina supplies. Effects and recipes are undecided.
- **Weaver:** a possible use for Wool/Cloth through fabric, padding, or clothing. Effects and recipes are undecided.

## Later progression idea

- **Experience for stockpile contributions:** if an EXP system is added later, contributing carried goods to the team stockpile should grant substantially more EXP than keeping them for personal crafting. EXP rules, rewards, and progression are not part of the first playable gather/carry/craft loop.
- **Experience for ram contributions:** when EXP is added, reward personal ram contributions according to goods actually consumed by a completed ram; the largest contributor should receive the largest ram-contribution EXP award. Refunded contributions should not generate EXP. The first playable siege uses operator priority instead of EXP as the immediate contributor recognition.

## Later monetization

- **Cosmetic equipment skins.** Later, offer visual skins for crossbows, battering rams, armor, and other equipment. Skins change appearance only, with no damage, range, speed, hitbox, visibility, or resource advantage. Catalog, pricing, acquisition, and release timing are undecided; do not build the store during map work.
